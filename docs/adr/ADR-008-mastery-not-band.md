# ADR-008: Keep mastery separate from IELTS band

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-17

## Context

The game measures narrow foundation skills and cannot reproduce certified IELTS assessment.

## Decision

Display internal mastery, accuracy, consistency, and coverage. Do not label them as official or estimated IELTS bands without an approved assessment method.

## Alternatives

- Convert mastery directly to band: misleading.

## Consequences

- Weekly Challenges measure internal progress.
- IELTS band remains externally verified or manually entered.
